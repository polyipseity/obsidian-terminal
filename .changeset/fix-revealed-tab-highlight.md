A terminal's tab no longer flashes a highlight when the terminal is opened, or when the "Focus on last terminal" or "Toggle focus on last terminal" command shows it. In the main area that highlight was drawn with notched corners. The highlight is also gone from terminal tabs in the sidebars. The tab of a note opened from a file link in the terminal no longer shows the highlight either. (by [@janah01](https://github.com/janah01))
