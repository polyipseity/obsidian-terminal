Terminal tabs and the "Open terminal" ribbon button have new icons. A new "Program status" setting under "Interface" shows on a terminal tab what its program reports with OSC 7501: idle, working, needs input, finished or error. Choose "None" (nothing shown), "Still" or "Animated" (the default). With "Still" or "Animated" the tab icon becomes a square with a raised border. "Animated" also moves the working dots, slides a changed tab title up and pops in a new finished, needs-input or error mark. Nothing moves where the system asks for reduced motion. (by [@janah01](https://github.com/janah01))
